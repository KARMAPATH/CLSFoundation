The Reality

Vulnerability is a choice we refuse to accept.

In today's world, a woman has the fundamental right to feel empowered and strong everywhere she goes. Yet, the personal security of women and children remains under constant threat.

Cheshta Learning System (CLS) Foundation was born from a refusal to accept this reality. We believe that to eradicate Occupational Sexism and general vulnerability, we must build a fortress of confidence from a young age.

Legal Empowerment

Beyond physical training, we conduct comprehensive workshops on Section 4 of The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013. We arm our youth with knowledge, making them aware of the magnitude of ignored problems to prevent assault.

Undeniable Proof of Impact

Acknowledged at the Highest Level.

Our methods aren't just theory. They are recognized by national leadership as critical to building a stronger, safer India.

From the PM's Desk

On 8 August 2019, our student Prerita received a personal birthday greeting and letter of appreciation from the office of the 14th Prime Minister of India, Shri Narendra Modi.

A Culture of Consistency: Prerita's Story

The letter commended Prerita's dedication and her role in motivating and encouraging her fellow students to enroll themselves in CLS's free self-defense classes and other outdoor activities.

It is this exact culture of discipline and peer-to-peer empowerment that CLS builds in every neighborhood we work in — and that earned recognition at the highest level of government.
